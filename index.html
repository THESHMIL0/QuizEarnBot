Frequently Asked Questions

Does the bot stop automatically when it reaches 25 gems? ▼

Yes! Both the Android Mobile APK and the Windows PC app actively read your screen's gem counter. As soon as you hit 25 gems, the bot immediately stops and sets status to "25 GEMS HIT", allowing you to safely claim your 1,000 Coins Super Offer without losing progress.

Why is the Mobile APK solving speed so fast? ▼

The Mobile APK runs natively inside Android OS as an Accessibility Service. Instead of relying on slow USB/ADB commands, it reads screen memory in 1 millisecond and dispatches touches in 50 milliseconds!

Is the Mobile APK safe to install? ▼

100% safe. The APK has ZERO internet permissions (it cannot even connect to the web), requires zero root access, and operates purely within local Android accessibility controls.

Can I run the Mobile APK inside BlueStacks? ▼

Yes! Drag and drop QuizEarnBot.apk into BlueStacks, turn on Accessibility for "Quiz Math Assistant", and you'll get the exact same instant 0.05s solving speed and floating mod ball on your PC!
