# Quiz & Earn Auto-Bot (v3.0)

Automated background solver, multi-instance gem farmer, and offer milestone auto-installer for **Quiz & Earn** (`com.quiz.earn.money.game`) and **CashMafia** (`com.rayole.cashmafia2`).

---

## 🚀 What's New in v3.0

### 1. Cyberpunk Sci-Fi Mod Menu HUD
- **Custom Sci-Fi Frame**: Chamfered 45° beveled corners, subtle scanlines, and glowing neon cyan corner brackets matching futuristic gaming overlays.
- **Direction Selector**:
  - `◀ LEFT`: Targets the **Super Offer (700 Coins)** on the left side of the home screen.
  - `RIGHT ▶`: Targets the **Mega Offer (800 Coins)** on the right side of the home screen.
- **Auto-Download Toggle**:
  - `ON`: Automatically launches the selected offer, watches the reward ad, downloads the target app from Google Play Store, runs engagement simulation, and collects the reward.
  - `OFF`: Pure gem solver & quiz farmer; preserves gems without initiating downloads.

### 2. Holographic Cyber Minimize Ball
- **Holographic Radar Rings**: Cyan tick marks and outer glowing orbital radar nodes.
- **Electric Cyan to Neon Lime Bezel**: Sweep gradient border transitioning from `#00E5FF` into `#76FF03` with a 3 o'clock inward chevron notch (`>`).
- **Bottom BOT Tab**: Inset capsule badge labeled **`BOT`** in glowing cyan.
- **Vector Robot Head**: High-tech metallic dome helmet, dual antennas, glowing forehead sensor gem, obsidian faceplate visor, and smiling neon lime green circular eyes.
- **Smooth Expand & Collapse**: Tap the floating ball to open the full mod menu; tap `—` in the menu to minimize back to the ball.

### 3. Offer Engine & Ad Resiliency
- **Mega & Super Offer Navigation**: Screen-priority detector prioritizing home cards, milestones, and gem collection before quiz questions.
- **Toolbar & Question Filtering**: Eliminates false positives from "Mega Quiz" toolbars and home coin counters (`700` / `800`).
- **Auto-Restart & Recovery**: Restarts the quiz app if reward video ads fail to load, bypassing ad stalls automatically.

## ⚡ Quick Start
1. Open BlueStacks 5 (Resolution: `900x1600` Portrait).
2. Enable Android Debug Bridge (ADB) in **BlueStacks Settings -> Advanced**.
3. Run `deploy_all.ps1` in PowerShell, or double-click `QuizEarnBot.exe`.
